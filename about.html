About Our Club

InnovateX Club is dedicated to students who are passionate about learning, building, and innovating with technology. Our club conducts workshops, events, and group activities that help students grow in coding, design, leadership, and teamwork. We encourage creativity, collaboration, and continuous improvement to help students explore their potential and create impactful projects.
